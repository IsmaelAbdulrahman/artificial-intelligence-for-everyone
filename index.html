The book

A complete course in artificial intelligence as it actually stands. It starts with what AI is and is not, works through how language models really function, then teaches how to use them well — and ends by building real systems, with retrieval, agents, guardrails and the arithmetic of what any of it costs.

  • The idea in plain words before any notation appears.
  • Every equation explained — each symbol named, with an "in words" line beside it.
  • A worked example with real numbers you can follow with a pencil.
  • Dated, checkable sources — 308 of them, and a re-check list for every figure that will age.
  • Runnable code for all of Part Four, and a matching lab in the app for every chapter.

The whole book on your device, plus a laboratory for every chapter. No account, no internet connection, no permissions at all — and nothing you do ever leaves your phone.
